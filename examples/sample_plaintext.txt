SUBJECT: NETWORK SECURITY

PROJECT TITLE: DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT OF A CLASSICAL CRYPTOGRAPHY TOOLKIT

This sample document demonstrates the encryption, integrity preservation, and decryption
capabilities of the Classical Cryptography Toolkit.

All eight supported ciphers (Caesar, Monoalphabetic, Playfair, Hill, Vigenère,
One-Time Pad, Rail Fence, and Columnar Transposition) can process this document
while preserving case, spaces, and punctuation where supported by the algorithm.
